All Services: Watch Several IPTV Services as One Library

Use several Xtream Codes, M3U and Emby services in Tachyon: switch on the Landing screen, or merge them into one Home, one channel list and one guide.

Getting Started24 October 20262 Min ReadTVPhoneDesktop

Have more than one service — say an Xtream account, an M3U playlist and your own Emby server? Tachyon lets you watch each one on its own, or all of them as one library.

The Landing Screen

Landing ("What Are We Watching?") is where Tachyon starts. It shows a tile for each service and, once you have two or more, All Services. Pick a tile and that service opens.

  • All Services merges every enabled service: one Home, one channel list, one TV guide, one Movies and one Series page.
  • A small service tag in the service's colour sits on every card, channel and row, so you always know where something comes from. Set each service's colour in its form.
  • Settings → Services → Startup → Ask Which Service to Watch at Startup shows Landing at every start; off, Tachyon goes back to your last choice.

Switching Services

DeviceWhere
TVThe left rail: Landing Screen, then the service right under it.
PhoneThe side menu (menu button, top left): the service in view opens into the list to change it.
ComputerThe rail on the left: Landing Screen, then the service right under it.

Each service remembers the category you were on in Live TV, Movies, Series and the TV Guide, so switching back puts you where you left off.

Order, Favorites and Refreshing

  • Move the service cards in Settings → Services up or down with their arrows (on a computer you can also drag them) to set the order. Landing and the service menu follow it.
  • Favorites follow your pick: with one service you see its favorites, and All Services combines them.
  • Refresh Library updates the service in view — or every service when All Services is open.

Turning a Service Off Without Removing It

Each service has an Enabled switch in its form. A disabled service stays saved with its login, but it's out of the app: off Landing and every page, and its library is never loaded or refreshed. Only Settings → Services still lists it, ready to enable again.

Tip

The same set of services on your TV, phone and computer? Set them up once on one device and use Service Sync on the others.

Questions and Answers

Does All Services use more connections?

No. Tachyon keeps to one stream per service at any moment, merged or not. See One Connection Per Service.

Why does a movie show once even though two services have it?

Group Versions of a Title (Settings → Appearance, on by default) shows copies of the same title as one poster; the title's page lists them under Available In. See Big Libraries.
