Reading the Stream Details: Tags and the Stream Info Line

What Tachyon's stream tags and the Stream Info line (Needs, Provider, Arriving, Buffered, Line) tell you, and where a slowdown comes from.

Help24 October 20264 Min ReadTVPhoneDesktop

When a channel stutters, the big question is why: your internet, the provider, or the device? Tachyon's player shows what it knows, so you can tell at a glance.

Where They Show

With the player's controls up — and for a few seconds after a channel starts — the header shows:

  • the Stream Info line: Needs · Provider · Arriving · Buffered · Line;
  • a row of stream tags under it: the picture, the sound and how it's delivered.

Both come with Settings → Playback → Show Stream Details (on by default). The Pin Stream Info button (a gauge) in the bottom bar pins the line on the picture; each press moves it, from the top to the bottom, or off. Channels, movies and series each keep their own spot.

The Stream Info Line

PartWhat It Means
NeedsHow fast the stream has to arrive to play smoothly: its own bitrate.
ProviderHow fast the provider sends it to you, measured from the stream itself. The letters after it are the country its server is in.
ArrivingWhat arrives this very moment. It jumps while the buffer fills; on a movie it reads Buffer Full once full, and From Storage while it plays a part already stored on the device.
BufferedSeconds of picture stored ahead of you — the cushion that rides out a hiccup.
LineYour internet, two ways, taking turns: your own line, and the route toward the stream's server. Dots: not measured yet.
The barGreen fills as far as the provider's speed reaches; the white mark is what the stream needs.
The LIVE stripGreen: smooth. Amber: a close call. Red: falling behind.

What It's Telling You

What You SeeMost LikelyTry
Provider well above Needs, Buffered holding or growingThe stream is fine.If the picture still stutters, it's the device: try another Decoder, or another version of the channel.
Provider below Needs, Line well above itThe provider (or the route to it) is too slow.Another version of the channel or title, later, or tell your provider.
Your line close to Needs or below itYour internet.Move closer to the Wi-Fi router or use a cable; pause other downloads at home.
Provider and Arriving stay at dots, Buffered stuck at 0 sNothing is coming from the provider.The channel may be off air: try another; if none play, check the service.
Your line is fast, but the route to another country is slowThe long way to the provider's server.A copy from a closer server, another time of day, or Deeper Buffer for Movies & Series.

The Stream Tags

Tags say only what the stream itself reported. If something isn't known, no tag is shown — a picture whose scan type wasn't reported shows FHD, never a guessed 1080p.

TagMeaning
1080p · 1080i · 720pDesktop Picture size; p = progressive, i = interlaced.
4K · QHD · FHD · HD · SDThe picture size (on Android always; on a computer when the stream didn't say progressive or interlaced).
H.264 · HEVC · AV1 · MPEG-2The video codec.
HDR10 · HLG · Dolby VisionA high-dynamic-range picture.
50 fpsFrames per second.
AAC · AC-3 · E-AC-3 · DTS · MP2The sound's codec.
2.0 · 5.1 · 7.1Sound channels.
3 TracksMore than one audio track — pick one with Audio.
TS · MP4 · MKVThe container: what the stream or file is.
HLS · DASH · RTSPHow it's delivered.
Desktop Index First · Index At End · Index CachedWhere a movie's index sits — at the end means a slower start; cached means this start skipped the wait.
HW Decoder · SW DecoderAndroid Hardware or software decoding.
Picture Repair · Audio → AACDesktop The picture or sound is converted on the fly.

Amber tags point to problems in the stream itself — Clock Jump on every device, and Desktop Slow Provider, Picture Gaps and Dead Audio Track — so you know the trouble comes from the provider, not your connection or the app. On Android, a slow provider shows in the LIVE strip and as a notice.

Tip

Desktop Stats for Nerds (I) shows even more: dropped frames, the bitrate, the buffer, live latency and auto-recoveries.

Questions and Answers

Where can I read this in the app?

About → How To → Read the Stream Info Line explains every part, with the same cases as above.

Do the speed checks use my provider connection?

No. Line is measured against a speed-test service, never against your provider, so it never takes a stream slot.
