Tip

The same keys move you around with a TV remote or a wireless keyboard on a computer connected to a TV. Turn on Settings → Appearance → Big-Screen Mode for the larger layout.

Questions and Answers

How do I see the shortcuts inside the app?

Press ? (or H) while something plays for the Keyboard Mapping over the picture, or open Settings → Shortcuts.

Why do ↑ and ↓ do different things on channels and movies?

On a live channel, ↑ ↓ open the channel list so you can zap; on a movie or episode there are no channels to browse, so they change the volume. Shift ↑ Shift ↓ change the volume on a channel.
