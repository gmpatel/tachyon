Back Up and Move Your Settings to a New Device

Save your services, settings, favorites and history to one encrypted file, and load it on a new phone, TV or computer — or after a reinstall.

Settings24 October 20262 Min ReadTVPhoneDesktop

A backup file holds your whole setup — services with their logins, settings, favorites, watch history and more — encrypted in one small .settings file. Load it on another device, or after reinstalling, and everything is as it was.

Make a Backup

  • Phone Settings → Data → Backup & Transfer → Export Settings, then choose where to save it. Cloud storage is handy for moving it to another device.
  • Desktop Settings → Data & Storage → Backup & Transfer → Export Settings.
  • TV A TV has no file picker, so use your phone: open Easy Settings and export from its Data tab. The file is saved on the phone.

The file is named after the version and the time, like Tachyon-1.3.5-…-20261024-093000.settings.

Load It

  1. On the other device: Settings → Data → Backup & Transfer → Import Settings (on a computer: Data & Storage; a TV: Easy Settings → Data).
  2. Pick the file.
  3. Tick the parts you want — every part is ticked to start.
  4. Merge adds them to what's there; Replace swaps what's there for the file's (on phones and TVs it asks first).

A part you leave out stays as it is. After the import, Tachyon lists anything it couldn't bring in.

A file made on a computer opens on a phone or TV and the other way round; the parts a device doesn't have are skipped. A file from an older or newer version of Tachyon brings in all it can.

Heads Up

The file holds your service logins (encrypted). Keep it to yourself, and don't post it anywhere.

Automatic Snapshots

Each version of Tachyon also keeps an encrypted snapshot of your setup on the device. Desktop When a fresh install finds one, it asks Bring Over Your Setup? — so an update or a reinstall on the same computer starts where you left off.

Backup File or Service Sync?

A backup is a copy of one moment, for a new device, a reinstall or safe keeping. To keep several devices on the same services all the time, use Service Sync instead — change a service on one device and the others follow.

Questions and Answers

Is my GDroid sign-in in the file?

No. An optional sign-in is never included in a backup.

Can I move my setup from a computer to a TV?

Yes. Export on the computer, copy the file to your phone, and import it on the TV through Easy Settings → Data.
