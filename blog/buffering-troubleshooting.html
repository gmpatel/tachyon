Buffering and Playback Problems: A Troubleshooting Guide

Fix buffering, freezing, no sound, black pictures and connection-limit messages in Tachyon — what each message means and what to try, step by step.

Help24 October 20264 Min ReadTVPhoneDesktop

Tachyon fixes most stream problems by itself while you watch: stalls, frozen pictures, broken clocks and dropped connections are noticed and handled, and a ring in the middle of the picture is all you usually see. When something still isn't right, this guide helps you find where the problem is — and what to do.

First: Where Is the Problem?

Show the player's controls and look at the Stream Info line (Needs · Provider · Arriving · Buffered · Line). It tells you whether the trouble is your internet, the provider or the device. See Reading the Stream Details.

Live Channels Buffer

  1. Check the badge at the start of the bottom bar. A red Slow Provider or Low Buffer points at the provider; Reconnecting at the connection.
  2. Try another version of the channel (HD, FHD, SD). Turn on Settings → Playback → Fall Back to Other Versions of a Channel to do it by itself when a channel keeps failing.
  3. Give it more cushion. Android Settings → Playback → Video Player → Buffer: Large or Very Large rides out shaky connections (channels take a moment longer to start). Desktop Buffering Profile → Stable or a longer Live Buffer.
  4. Your own network: move closer to the Wi-Fi router, use a cable, and pause other downloads or streams at home.
Good to Know

Some providers send a channel slower than real time. No player can play that without pauses; Tachyon makes them fewer and longer instead of stopping every few seconds, and says so.

Movies Stop to Buffer

  • When a provider sends a title slower than it plays, the player says by how much and offers Buffer Ahead: it fills enough, once, to watch to the end without stops, with a countdown.
  • Settings → Playback → Deeper Buffer for Movies & Series makes each refill longer: one longer pause instead of many short ones.
  • Movie Index Cache (All Titles) makes a title you open again start without the index wait.

No Sound on Some Channels

  • Android Set Settings → Playback → Video Player → Surround Passthrough to Off if your TV or receiver doesn't take Dolby or DTS.
  • Try another track with the Audio button: some channels' first track is empty (Tachyon usually switches by itself).
  • Desktop Dolby, DTS and MP2 sound is converted automatically; the first start can take a moment longer.
  • Check the volume isn't muted — the Volume button in the player mutes with one press.

Black or Frozen Picture With Sound

  • Android Player Options → Decoder → Prefer Software for that channel. Tachyon also tries this by itself once.
  • Player Options → Refresh Stream (on a computer: R).
  • Try another version of the channel.

"Subscription Expired" When You Play

Tachyon knows the subscription has ended, so it asks before playing: Try Anyway checks the account again (if it's renewed, it plays), Edit Service opens its settings, and Cancel closes the question.

Still Stuck? Report It

When you report a problem, give the version and build shown in Settings → About, the kind of service, and what you saw. Desktop Settings → About → System & Diagnostics → Export Diagnostics makes one file to send along: passwords, tokens and e-mail addresses are masked, and titles aren't recorded.

Questions and Answers

Why does a channel take a few seconds to open on a one-connection account?

Many panels keep counting the last stream for a few seconds after it closed. Tachyon quietly asks again for about 8 seconds before it shows a connection-limit message. See One Connection Per Service.

Does a faster internet plan fix buffering?

Only when Line is the slow part. If Provider is below Needs while your line is fast, the provider's server is the bottleneck.
