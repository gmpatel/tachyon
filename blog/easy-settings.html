Easy Settings: Change Your TV's Settings From Your Phone

Scan the QR code on your TV and every Tachyon setting opens on your phone. Add services with a real keyboard, back up the TV and save straight to it.

Settings24 October 20263 Min ReadTVPhone

Typing a server address, a username and a long password with a TV remote is nobody's idea of fun. Easy Settings puts the TV's settings on your phone (or a computer): scan a QR code on the TV, and the whole of Tachyon's Settings opens in the phone's browser. Everything you change there is saved straight to the TV.

Good to Know

Easy Settings is part of the Tachyon app on TVs (Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV). Your phone needs only a web browser — no app to install.

Connect Your Phone

  1. On the TV, open Settings → About, or press Easy Settings at the top of any Settings page.
  2. Put your phone on the same Wi-Fi as the TV.
  3. Scan the QR code with the phone's camera. No camera handy? Type the address shown beside the code into any browser.
  4. The page opens on Services; the tabs at the bottom have the rest.

The tile on the About page shows a dot: orange while it waits for a phone, green once a phone is connected.

What You Can Do There

  • Change any setting, on the TV's own tabs: Appearance, Data, Parental, Playback, Services and Subtitles. It saves to the TV at once, and changes made on the TV show up on the phone within seconds.
  • Add, edit, reorder or remove services with your phone's keyboard. A service's More Options has the same TV Guide and Advanced options as the TV's form. Passwords stay hidden unless you tap the eye.
  • Service Sync: set the TV up as the main device or sync it from one, on the Services tab. See Service Sync.
  • Back up and restore the TV: the Data tab exports the TV's settings file to your phone, or imports one into the TV — handy, as a TV has no file picker. See Back Up and Move Your Settings.
  • Subtitles: the subtitle look with a live preview, and the logins or API keys for the online subtitle services.

Every page of Easy Settings has its own address, so you can keep a link to, say, the Services tab.

Safe by Design

  • The page only works while the TV shows its About page. Leave it and the TV stops answering. If a phone is connected, the TV asks Leave Easy Settings? before you go.
  • The address holds a code that changes every day. New Code on the TV changes it at once, and a phone on the old code has to scan again.
  • Only devices on your home network can open it, and a device that keeps guessing codes is shut out.
  • With App Lock set, the parental settings ask for its PIN on the phone too.

Questions and Answers

The page doesn't open. What should I check?

Check that the phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the TV (not on mobile data or a guest network), and that the TV is still showing its About page. If the code changed (a new day, or New Code), scan again.

Does it work from a computer?

Yes. Type the address shown on the TV into a browser on a computer on the same network.

Does my phone need the Tachyon app?

No. Any web browser works. The QR code opens a Tachyon web page first, which then goes straight to your TV.
