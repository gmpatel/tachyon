Watch Australian Live TV, Free

ABC, SBS, Seven, Nine and 10, live with a TV guide, right next to your other services. Copy one link for your city and add it to Tachyon as an M3U playlist. It takes about a minute.

  1. Add Service → M3U Playlist → Playlist Link
  2. Paste your city’s link into Playlist URL
  3. Select Add Service. The TV guide loads by itself.

Quicker in the app (1.1.2 and newer): Settings → About → How To → Watch Australian Live TV, then Add Aussie Live. No typing, the easiest way on a TV. See How

Features

Everything You Watch, Without the Wait

One app for every service you have, made to feel instant on a phone and on the big screen.

All Your Services, One Place

Add Xtream Codes, M3U and Emby services and switch between them, or browse everything merged into one library.

Live TV and a Real Guide

What's on now, a full XMLTV guide with categories and reminders, and catch-up or Start Over where your provider supports it.

Movies & Series, Done Right

Posters, ratings, cast & crew, seasons, Next Up and Continue Watching. Copies of the same title are grouped into one card.

Built for Speed

Libraries of 100,000+ titles load in the background while you browse, and the next start opens straight from a compact on-device cache.

Self-Healing Playback

Stalls, frozen pictures and network drops are spotted and recovered automatically, so you keep watching instead of restarting.

One Stream per Service

Tachyon never opens a second connection, so shared and single-connection plans keep working, even while it loads details in the background.

Made for the Big Screen

A 10-foot interface that works with the simplest remote: D-pad, OK and Back. Hold OK for options, zap with up / down.

Subtitles & Tracks

Pick audio and subtitle tracks before you press Play, and search OpenSubtitles, SubDL and Podnapisi with your own account.

Private by Design

No account needed, no ad SDKs, no trackers. Your services, logins, favorites and history stay on your device.

Android TV · Google TV · Fire TV

A TV App That Respects Your Remote

Everything is reachable with up, down, left, right, OK and Back. No pointer, no hunting for tiny buttons.

  • Zap like a set-top box: up / down changes channel, with a channel list one press away.
  • Hold OK for options on any card: favorite, versions, details.
  • Google TV Continue Watching: pick up where you left off straight from the home screen.
  • Starts on your last channel if you like, and keeps a parental PIN on adult categories.
Phones & Tablets

Your Whole Library in Your Pocket

The same services and favorites, laid out for one hand, with a full-screen player when you want it.

  • Search everything across all your services at once.
  • Swipe gestures in the player: seek, volume, brightness, and channel browsing on live TV.
  • Seasons, episodes and Next Up with watched marks that follow you.
  • Picture-in-picture so a match keeps playing while you check something else.
The Player

Plays What Others Stumble On

Hardware-accelerated playback with a software fallback, tuned for real-world IPTV streams.

  • HLS, MPEG-TS, MP4, MKV and more, with picture repair for broken streams.
  • Stream details on screen: resolution, codecs, frame rate and decoder, at a glance.
  • Volume Leveling evens out loud adverts and quiet films.
  • Watched marks at 90 %, resume points, and the next episode lined up.

FAQ

Good Questions

Does Tachyon come with channels or movies?

No. Tachyon is a media player only. It doesn't provide, host, sell or recommend any content, and it isn't affiliated with any IPTV provider. You add the services you're entitled to use. A built-in offline demo shows how everything works first.

Is it free?

Yes: Freeware Forever, with no subscriptions and No UI Ads.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets, Android TV, Google TV (including Chromecast with Google TV) and Fire TV, on Android 7.0 or newer. It's tuned to stay smooth on low-cost TV boxes with little memory.

Which services can I add?

Xtream Codes accounts (server, username and password), M3U / M3U8 playlist links (or an M3U built from a panel login), and Emby servers. You can add several and switch between them, or browse them merged.

How do I update?

From Google Play, it updates itself. Installed with a Downloader code or the APK? Repeat the same step: the code always installs the newest Tachyon over your copy, and your services and settings stay.

Why does Google Drive warn that it can't scan the file?

Google Drive says this about every app file of this size. Select Download Anyway: the file is the official Tachyon app, signed with the same key as every release.

Android says "App not installed". What now?

Usually a copy from another source (for example Google Play versus a code) is already installed with a different signature. Keep updating the way you first installed, or uninstall that copy first (this removes its services and settings).

Will it open a second connection on my plan?

No. Tachyon keeps to one stream per service, so single-connection and shared plans keep working.
