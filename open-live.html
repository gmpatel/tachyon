Free Live TV

Try Open Live TV

Free live TV playlists, kept by independent projects, that work in Tachyon. They are examples to try, not recommendations. Tachyon provides no channels and doesn't check these lists, and they can change or stop at any time.

In the app: About → How To → Try Open Live TV. Each list has an Add button that adds it as an M3U service. Lists for your country come first. To add one by hand: Add Service → M3U Playlist → Playlist Link, then paste the link. The TV guide loads by itself when the list names one.

Good to Know

  • Risky means the list isn't made by the channels' owners: app channels re-listed by a third party, or a volunteer list where not every stream comes from its broadcaster. Use your own judgment.
  • Kiwi Live shows only on a New Zealand connection. Its main networks (Three, Sky Open, Bravo, eden, RUSH) stop everywhere else.
  • Many channels only play in their own country, and a VPN set to another country stops them.
  • Free TV marks some channels as YouTube links. Those don't play in Tachyon.
  • Big lists (iptv-org, all countries: about 11,000 channels) take longer to load, and a fair share of them are dead at any moment. A country's list is quicker.
  • Australians: the Aussie Live card, first in the same popup, adds your city's free-to-air channels. See Watch Australian Live TV.
  • Tachyon checks a list answers before it adds it, and asks whether to remove an added list that stops loading.
  • From your phone: scan Easy Settings on the TV's About page and open the Open TV tab.
